

# 3D AI Student Portfolio — Clean & Professional

A polished, LinkedIn-friendly portfolio website showcasing your AI/ML expertise with subtle 3D visual elements for a modern tech feel.

---

## 1. Hero Section
- Clean animated hero with your name, title ("AI Student / ML Engineer"), and a short tagline
- Subtle 3D floating geometric shapes (cubes, spheres, neural network nodes) using CSS 3D transforms and animations
- Call-to-action buttons: "View Projects" and "Download Resume"
- Professional profile photo area

## 2. About Me
- Brief bio highlighting your passion for AI/ML
- Key stats (e.g., projects completed, technologies known, papers published)
- Clean card layout with smooth scroll-in animations

## 3. Skills & Education
- Visual skill bars or cards for AI/ML technologies (Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, etc.)
- Education timeline showing your academic journey
- Certifications or courses section

## 4. Projects Showcase
- Grid of project cards with hover effects showing 3D tilt/flip animations
- Each card: thumbnail, title, description, tech stack tags, and links (GitHub, live demo)
- Filter by category (e.g., NLP, Computer Vision, Deep Learning)

## 5. Blog / Research
- Clean list of articles or research papers
- Each entry: title, date, brief abstract, and a "Read More" link
- Tags for topics (e.g., Transformers, GANs, Reinforcement Learning)

## 6. Resume Download
- Dedicated section with a preview of your resume
- One-click download button (PDF)
- Option to view key highlights inline

## 7. Contact
- Simple contact form (name, email, message)
- Social links: LinkedIn, GitHub, email
- Clean footer with navigation links

## Design & Experience
- **Color scheme**: Dark navy/charcoal with subtle blue accents — professional yet techy
- **Typography**: Modern sans-serif fonts (clean and readable)
- **3D effects**: CSS-based 3D transforms for card hovers, floating hero elements, and parallax scrolling — no heavy 3D libraries needed
- **Animations**: Smooth fade-ins and slide-ups on scroll
- **Responsive**: Fully mobile-friendly for LinkedIn mobile viewers
- **Static content**: All content hardcoded (no database needed), easy to customize
